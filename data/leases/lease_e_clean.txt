RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (SYNTHETIC SAMPLE, NOT A REAL DOCUMENT)

SECTION 1. PARTIES AND PREMISES. This Lease is made between Juniper House LLC ("Landlord") and Sam Okafor ("Tenant") for the residential unit at 9 Juniper Lane, Unit 2, Fairview (the "Premises"). The Premises are leased for use as a private residence only.

SECTION 2. TERM AND RENEWAL. The term of this Lease begins on May 1, 2027 and ends on April 30, 2028. At the end of the term, the tenancy converts to a month-to-month tenancy unless either party gives the other at least 30 days' written notice.

SECTION 3. RENT AND PAYMENT. Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $2,200, due on the first day of each month. Rent received after the 5th day of the month is subject to a late fee of 5% of the monthly rent. Payment may be made by electronic transfer or by check delivered to the management office.

SECTION 4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $2,200 before taking possession. Landlord shall return the deposit, less lawful deductions for unpaid rent and damage beyond normal wear and tear, within 21 days after Tenant vacates and returns all keys.

SECTION 5. UTILITIES AND SERVICES. Tenant shall pay for electricity and internet service used at the Premises and shall place those accounts in Tenant's name. Landlord shall pay for water, sewer, and trash service.

SECTION 6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant shall keep the Premises clean and shall promptly report any needed repairs to Landlord in writing. Landlord shall make repairs to the plumbing, heating, and electrical systems at Landlord's expense, except for damage caused by Tenant.

SECTION 7. LANDLORD ENTRY. Landlord may enter the Premises to inspect, make repairs, or show the unit to prospective tenants after giving Tenant at least 24 hours' notice. In an emergency, Landlord may enter without notice.

SECTION 8. OCCUPANCY AND GUESTS. Only Tenant and those persons listed on the application may reside in the Premises. Guests may stay for up to 14 consecutive days without Landlord approval.

SECTION 9. PETS. No animals may be kept in the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent.

SECTION 10. SUBLETTING AND ASSIGNMENT. Tenant shall not sublet the Premises or assign this Lease without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

SECTION 11. INSURANCE. Tenant shall maintain renter's insurance with at least $100,000 of liability coverage throughout the term. Tenant shall deliver proof of renter's insurance to Landlord within 10 days after the start of the term.

SECTION 12. MOVE-IN INSPECTION. Landlord and Tenant shall inspect the Premises together at move-in. Tenant must return the signed move-in condition checklist to Landlord within 7 days after move-in; otherwise the Premises are deemed to be in good condition.

SECTION 13. EARLY TERMINATION. Tenant may terminate this Lease before the end of the term by giving 60 days' written notice and paying a termination fee equal to two months' rent.

SECTION 14. DEFAULT AND LATE CHARGES. If Tenant fails to pay rent or violates this Lease, Landlord may give written notice of default and a reasonable period to cure.

SECTION 15. DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND FEES. The parties shall first try to resolve any dispute through good-faith discussion.

SECTION 16. MOVE-OUT. At move-out, Tenant shall return the Premises in the same condition as at move-in, except for normal wear and tear, and shall return all keys. Tenant shall provide a forwarding address in writing.

SECTION 17. GENERAL PROVISIONS. This Lease is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in a writing signed by both. Notices must be in writing and delivered to the address listed above.
